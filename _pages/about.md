<p style="text-align: justify;">
I am Xiaopeng Xi, an Assistant Professor at SolBridge International School of Business. My research interests include condition monitoring, fault diagnosis, and prognostics. I have led or participated in more than 10 research projects. The application scenarios contain blast furnace, high speed train, generator set, etc. I have published more than 40 research papers, including the SCI papers in IEEE Transactions on Reliability, Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing, etc. I was awarded the Natural Science Award of the Chinese Association of Automation in 2019.
</p>
